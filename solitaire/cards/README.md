# Solitaire's card faces

The 52 faces, `<rank><suit>.svg` (rank `A`, `2` to `9`, `T`, `J`, `Q`, `K`; suit `S`, `H`, `D`, `C`), are Adrian Kennard's
SVG playing cards, from https://www.me.uk/cards/standard/ (fetched 2026-11-19). He released them "under a completely open
Creative Commons CC0 Public Domain licence, not even requiring attribution"
(https://revk.uk/2018/06/svg-vector-playing-cards.html); the court cards are his tracings of a Goodall & Son deck of about
1870. Source: https://codeberg.org/RevK/SVG-playing-cards. The back, `back.svg`, is ours (CC BY 4.0, as the site's other
drawings).

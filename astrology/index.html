This app needs JavaScript, and a page served over http (python3 -m http.server, then localhost).

The vocabulary every module sees (astrology/rules/preamble.hz). The listings are text, highlighted, until Edit in Hazel opens each in Hazel's own editor, where Ctrl+click on a name, or F12, goes to its definition; Show as text goes back, your edits kept. The astronomy is rewritten from astronomia (MIT), after Jean Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, and Paul Schlyter's planetary positions.

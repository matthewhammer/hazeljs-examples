Mahjong solitaire, its rules in Hazel

Hazel activities › One-player games · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

144 tiles stacked in layers. Take away two tiles that match, as long as each is free: nothing on top of it, and nothing beside it on its left or on its right. Two tiles match when their faces are the same; any flower matches any flower, and any season any season. Clear the table to win. Every deal can be won: Hazel makes it by playing it. Stuck? ↩️ takes a pair back, 🔀 shuffles the tiles still out (and keeps the game winnable), ❓ shows a pair Hazel would take.

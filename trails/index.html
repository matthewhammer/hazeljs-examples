Light trails, against Hazel

Hazel activities › Hazel AI opponents · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

You ride the blue light, Hazel the orange one; each leaves a wall of light behind it. Turn with the arrow keys (or W A S D), a swipe, or the four buttons: the first to run into the edge or a trail loses the round, and both at once is a draw. First to three rounds wins. Each step of a round, and Hazel's rider (and yours, when 🤖 Hazel plays for you), are Hazel (trails/rules/Trails.hz). ⏸ or the space bar pauses.

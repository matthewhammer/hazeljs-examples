Gnome world, planned in Hazel

Hazel activities › One-player games · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

You are a gnome in a world sixteen screens across and eight down, from the deep rock up to the tops of great trees: run with the arrow keys (or A and D), jump or climb with ↑ (or W), go down or dig down with ↓ (or S), or use the buttons. Push into the ground to dig it (a timber set goes in as you dig along, a ladder as you dig up or down); 🪵 or B builds: at the edge of a pit or the water, a plank ahead, a bridge across; in a pit, a ladder up, to climb out by. Push into the ground to dig it: soil and sand quickly, clay and sandstone slowly, shale slowest; limestone and granite you cannot dig. Gems glitter in the rock (💎). In a tunnel you have dug you can climb any way; up a great tree, climb its ladders, or the stair inside its trunk, out along its limbs to the tree houses (walk in at the door) and across the rope bridges; in one tree house waits the tree-climbing pack 🧗, and with it you can climb any tree, like a squirrel. Under a hill, deep down, is the gnomes' village: a door on the hill, a ladder down, and rooms, furnished, with chimneys, light shafts and air shafts up to the meadow. The creeks, the stream and the river: the beavers fell the willows and aspens, drag them to their dams, and each log raises the water, and the water in the ground beside it. The water in the ground shows as blue in the soil and stone, darker the fuller, and seeps down, slowly, faster where roots go, until it stands on clay or shale: a bowl of clay holds it. Under the water, hold your breath (the bubbles). The world's plan, its layers of rock, its rivers, villages, great trees, tree houses in sixteen styles and the beavers' minds are Hazel (world/rules/World.hz). ⏸ or the space bar pauses. ⛶ fills the screen with the world.

The pictures are inspired by Rien Poortvliet's paintings for Gnomes (Wil Huygen and Rien Poortvliet, 1976): the woods, the gnomes' homes among the roots, the earth in cutaway. They are our own drawings; none of his is copied. The ground, its roots and its mycelium are Gnome dig's; the trees and their leaf pads, Messed Up Dress Up's.

Gnome world, planned in Hazel

Hazel activities › One-player games · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

A gnome in a world thirty-two screens wide, from deep rock to the tops of great trees: Dad, Mom, the girl or the boy. ← → run · 💨 Shift held, dash · ↑ jump, climb · ↓ dig down, or slide down a slope · ⛏️ X dig the way you face · 🪵 B plank, bridge, ladder · 🐇🧞🐈 R on or off, with ← Clover, → the magic carpet, ↑ the cat tail (jump, then keep pressing jump in the air, or hold it, to fly up); R alone, a choice · space pause · F frame times · 🎮 a game controller: its arrows run, jump and climb, its buttons the rest.

Find the diving gear 🤿, the climbing pack 🧗, the running shoes 👟, the swim flippers 🏊, the super climbing gear 🪝, the cat tail 🐈, the gem finder 🧭 and the diggers ⛏️ ⚒️; dig for gems 💎; say hello to the villagers. High in the crown of the great tree farthest right, a swirling vortex 🌀 goes to 🌈 The rainbow sky, N's level: no digging there; caves to explore by their tunnels, the magic carpet 🧞 in its gnome house, ten cat head gems 🐱 that make the cat tail, and rainbow clouds to fly into for points. Its own vortex, at the far left, goes home. 💾 Save keeps the world as it is in a file, and 📂 Load brings it back; 🔄 Restart begins this world again; a reload, or a newer version, brings it back as you left it. 🧩 Load-merge brings a save from an older version into today's world: your digging, the gems you found and your things, with the new gems still to find. 📖 The instruction booklet, to print as a zine. The world, its water, its beavers and every step of the gnome are Hazel. Pictures after Rien Poortvliet's Gnomes (1976), our own drawings.

Edit in Hazel to change them.

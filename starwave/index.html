Star wave, its rules in Hazel

Hazel activities › One-player games · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

Wave after wave of invaders march across the sky and step down at each edge, faster as there are fewer of them, dropping bombs. Move your cannon with ⬅️ ➡️ (or drag along the field) and fire with ⬆️, Enter or a tap, one shot at a time; the space bar pauses. The four shields wear away under bombs and shots alike. Three lives; the game ends if the invaders reach the ground. Each step, and the robot that plays it for you, are Hazel (starwave/rules/StarWave.hz).

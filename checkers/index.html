Checkers, against Hazel

Hazel activities › Hazel AI opponents · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

You play black, from the bottom, and move first; Hazel plays white. A piece moves one square diagonally forward. Jump over one of Hazel's pieces onto the empty square beyond to take it; when you can jump, you must, and you keep jumping while you can. A piece that reaches the far row is crowned a king (👑), and moves backward too. Whoever cannot move loses. Tap a piece, then where it goes. The moves, the jumps, the kings and Hazel's choice are a Hazel program, shown beside the board: Hazel tries every move, then your every answer, and so on, as many moves ahead as you choose.

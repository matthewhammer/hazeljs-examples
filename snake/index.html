Snake, its rules in Hazel

Hazel activities › One-player games · source on GitHub · hazeljs on npm

Steer the snake to the food: the arrow keys (or W A S D), a swipe on the board, or the four buttons. Each meal makes it three cells longer and is 10 points; running into a wall, or into itself, ends the game (unless the walls wrap round, so you come out on the other side). Choose how fast it goes. Each step of the game, and the robot that plays it for you, are Hazel (snake/rules/Snake.hz). ⏸ or the space bar pauses.
